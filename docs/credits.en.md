# Credits

CS Retro would not be possible without the work of many other people.
We do not claim their work as our own. Original AUTHORS, CREDITS and license files remain
in place; this overview supplements them.

## Game and technical foundations

- **Valve and the original Half-Life/Counter-Strike developers:** game, content and SDK.
- **Unkle Mike, Flying With Gauss and Xash3D-FWGS contributors:** engine.
- **CS-NextClient and contributors:** functional client and GameUI sources.
- **Velaron/cs16-client and contributors:** Xash-compatible client body.
- **ReHLDS/ReGameDLL_CS and contributors:** gameplay and server logic.
- **FuryBaM/cs16-goldsrc-client and contributors:** menu/VGUI comparisons.
- **languagelawyer:** Molotov/inferno and menu references.
- **Noto Project Authors:** Noto Sans and Noto Sans Mono.
- **Xonotic Team:** announcer voices for the freeze countdown (GPL; `sound/announcer/`).
